# animation rules (plate + patch doctrine)

Animation is a sequence of redrawn pixel COMPOSITIONS, never a
picture being smoothly moved. quality ≈ clear poses × coherent
clusters × good timing. Frame count barely matters.

- PLATES STAY STILL; motion ships as small REGION PATCHES (redrawn
  cluster cels) applied over the still by a timeline. Never
  full-frame sequences — memory law.
- ANIMATE SHAPES, NOT PIXELS: a wrinkle, eyelid, finger, or flame is
  a connected cluster; the next frame contains a NEW whole cluster.
  Stable dither — animated noise (pixel boil) is banned.
- ONE VERB per animation (contract, reach, settle, drag, blink,
  drip, lurch). "Idle" is not a verb.
- KEY POSES FIRST, readable as stills; transitional frames only if
  earned. Ease by SPACING (0,0,1,2,4,7), not interpolation; the
  violent jerk is 0,0,0,4,4,4. Long holds beat many frames.
- WHOLE LOGICAL PIXELS at native grid; upscale only at display.
  Canvas transforms only for whole-layer translation/shake; every
  deformation or rotation is REDRAWN.
- VOLUME CONSERVED (lost top contour → gained bottom contour);
  deliberate squash redistributes, it never shrinks.
- CONTACT POINTS ANCHOR (palm/floor, cheek/wall, scalp/ceiling):
  stationary until they deliberately slip; motion bends around them.
- SECONDARY MOTION LAGS: fingers → palm anchors → forearm →
  shoulder → head deforms → dust after → saliva settles.
- MOTION HIERARCHY, one tier dominant: primary pose · secondary
  flesh/recoil · ambient flame/dust · micro glint. Per the
  stillness law, large static regions make small motion powerful.
- OUTLINES ARE STRUCTURAL and move with the pose; fills never move
  under a stale outline. Form light travels with the part;
  environmental light stays with its source; speculars may jump.
- CONSEQUENCES PERSIST: a lurch keeps its pixels, a brick stays
  displaced, a drop stays on the floor. Events, not loops.
- HIDDEN SEAMS: rest → contract → settle → LONG rest; multiple
  tracks on different periods so they rarely synchronize.
- TEST AT GAMEPLAY SIZE: motion visible only under zoom is texture,
  not animation.

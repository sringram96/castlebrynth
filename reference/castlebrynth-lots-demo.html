how to play

1 · See its attack. The enemy always shows its next attack before you roll.

2 · Roll 6 dice. Tap dice to keep them, then re-roll the rest once.

3 · Make combos. Select dice and attack — pair, run, full house, two pair... You can claim several combos in one turn; each die counts once. Dice that fit nothing go unused.

4 · Each combo works once per fight. The list at the bottom crosses off as you spend it.

5 · Your armor blocks 3 of every attack, automatically. Items are listed at the bottom — tap one to see what it does.

# TRUTH.md - SPOILERS (v2). The whole truth, tiered.

## the truth (T3)

The protagonist's baby brother was taken by the cult to be made the
vessel of the Morning King - Satan, plainly, in this document and
nowhere else below T3. The rite does not kill the child; it *replaces*
him, stage by stage. The protagonist pursued the takers to the portal
and stepped through knowing the toll: crossing costs memory. The
amnesia was chosen - they forgot the child, the pursuit, and everything
they were, to keep following. What remains is compulsion: the arms
remember the weight.

**The past is unwritten.** Whether the protagonist was once of the
cult is deliberately indeterminate at the start and is COLLAPSED BY
PLAY: the hidden complicity ledger (rites performed, robes worn, others
spent) plus key choices determine which history is true. The ending
names it. No content may resolve it earlier.

**Endings (final chamber):** read from the complicity ledger and the
final-depth choices - spectrum from severance (the child carried back
through a portal that takes its toll again) to inheritance (the cult
was right to half-recognize you). A false-rescue midpoint beat is
DEFERRED - designed later, not scheduled.
The Communion is never an ending; sanity break always wakes at the
Crossing (GAME.md #communion).

## what each tier may carry

- T0: physical residue only. Never "child"/"brother".
- T1: the deliberate crossing; the memory toll; half-recognition.
- T2: the infant, the rite's purpose, the escalating glimpses; the
  both-readings rule on your past is load-bearing.
- T3: everything above, plus the plain name and the endings.

## invariants

- The child never speaks and is never shown harmed - only changed,
  still by still, one glimpse per depth, a second voice under the cry.
- No content states the protagonist's past before the ending does.
- Cultist recognition lines scale with the ledger but never explain
  themselves.

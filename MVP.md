# MVP.md — the ratified minimum (slice = spec)

The playable slice (mock/slice-mvp.html) IS the specification. Ship
parity with it through the factory, nothing more.

## scope — eight pillars

1. **Five authored rooms, two depths:** the Crossing · a corridor slot
   with two variants (crooked / flooded) · the Sealed Gate · the Lower
   Hall · the Warden's Chamber.
2. **Frame:** full-bleed scene, top writing, pager panel ◂ ACTIONS ·
   SKILLS · ITEMS ▸, permanent strip (hp · might · will · ◎ · depth).
3. **The atom + investigation:** free tap always; risky actions cost.
4. **The sigil puzzle** gates depth 1→2: order discoverable from free
   taps (passage clue + lintel clue); wrong press resets and costs 1
   sanity; solving grants the Sigil Brand (✦ key item).
5. **Combat:** opposed d6+Might, armor soak, visible pursuit, free
   flee. **Ward** (learned from the rite-page): post-roll spend to
   negate a wound, uses per life. **The Warden:** intent telegraphed
   each round; at half health, the parry QTE.
6. **Grants:** the quiet font (+1 Might, free) and the black altar
   (+1 Might for −1 max Sanity). Persist through death.
7. **Death (wipe rule):** wake at the Crossing; lose everything except
   ✦ key items, *kept* items (the rusted knife), and one random
   survivor; consumables + tithes gone; flags/skills/grants persist;
   corridor variant swaps.
8. **Sanity as vignette only** — damaged by mistakes and investigation;
   zero = death by the same rule. No Communion sequence in MVP.

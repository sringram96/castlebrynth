# H005 — what does a `goto` target resolve to?

H005 must make "goto targets exist" checkable, and its acceptance
requires both that lint pass on the repo's content and that a seeded
"dangling goto" fixture fail. Those two cannot both hold under any
reading I can find, because nothing in the repo declares a namespace a
door ref could be resolved against:

- VOCAB.md: `goto: door_ref — forward-only; doors bound by the draw
  engine`; `src/core/cards.ts` says in as many words "a door ref, not a
  scene id". So resolving a goto against scene ids is the wrong law.
- `content/shore.yaml` (H003, landed) is the whole content set, and its
  only goto is `goto: down`. No scene `down` exists, and none is coming:
  H111 authors `crossing` and `corridor`. So the scene-id reading turns
  the repo's own content red on day one, and permanently.
- Nothing else declares doors. `CardScene` has exactly `scene, tier,
  enter, line, objects` — there is no place to author a door, and
  skill:content's "rooms declare door SENSES" has no schema behind it.
  GENERATOR.md (superseded) says exits are logical sockets the generator
  binds at layout time, i.e. unresolvable at lint time.

That leaves the check either wrong (scene ids), vacuous (nothing to
resolve against, so no fixture can fail), or dependent on a word I would
have to invent. Which do you want?

1. **Door refs are declared in content.** Add a `doors:` key to the
   scene schema and lint gotos against it — an H003 schema amendment
   plus an edit to `content/shore.yaml`, neither in H005's scope.
2. **Door refs are scene ids.** Then `content/shore.yaml` must change
   (`goto: down` → a real scene, or a scene `down` gets authored), and
   VOCAB.md's "not a scene id" gloss needs rewording. Also outside
   H005's scope.
3. **Door refs stay unresolvable in P0.** Drop "goto targets exist" and
   its fixture from H005's goal and acceptance; the linter ships the
   other four checks (gate satisfiability, mandatory fallback, refuse
   purity, banned words) and gotos are checked when the draw engine
   lands.

I did not guess, and I did not weaken the fixture to fit: nothing else
in the card is blocked, but the acceptance is one statement and I cannot
make it green without an answer here.

# H006 — three VOCAB words have nowhere to land

H006's acceptance is one statement, and one clause of it is "every VOCAB
delta observably applies". Eleven of VOCAB.md's fourteen words apply and
are observable. Three cannot, against the contract H001 froze — and two
of those three are not ambiguous, they are mechanically impossible. I
can implement neither without editing `src/core/types.ts`, which is
H001's scope, not this card's.

The frozen surfaces, as landed:

- `GameState` = `seed, flags, journal, refused, scene, items, rng,
  bundle`. Eight fields; `tests/acceptance/H001.types.test.ts` asserts
  that key set by name, and `isGameState` in `src/core/save.ts` (H007)
  validates the same eight as "every field the contract names".
- `Effect` = `say | journal | goto | prompt | fight | end`. Six kinds.

`src/core/types.ts` says the words outside `Effect` are "observable on
state instead". For these three that is not true.

## 1. `adjust: {stat|tithes, amount, clamped}` — nowhere to store, nowhere to surface

`AdjustTarget` is `StatId | "tithes"`. GameState has no stat, no tithes,
and no bounds for `clamped` to clamp against; `Effect` has no `adjust`
kind. So the delta can neither be written to state nor handed to the
shell. Any implementation is a no-op, and a no-op is not "observably
applies". GAME.md #frame names the strip — HP · Might · Will · ◎ tithes
· depth — but no field carries any of it.

## 2. `addObject: {scene, object, cause}` — the delta cannot construct what it adds

An object in a scene is a `SceneObject`: `{id, label?, line, actions}`.
`line` is mandatory — it is the free tap, and GAME.md #descent gives
every object one. The delta carries an **id and a cause, and no
definition**. There is no prose to show and no actions to offer, so
`addObject` cannot build the object it names.

Nor is there a catalogue to source one from. `CardObject` is exactly
`{tap, actions}` (`src/core/cards.ts`) — nothing marks an object as
authored-but-not-yet-present, so no scene declares the thing that would
later arrive. Sourcing the definition from elsewhere in the bundle would
mean inventing a rule: which scene it comes from, what happens when two
scenes define that id, what happens when none do. That is law, and I
would be making it up.

This is a regression, not an oversight in my reading. The pre-`Start
over` engine had both halves: `CardObject` carried `hidden`, so content
could author an absent object, and that VOCAB.md documented the fold
rule in as many words — "the scene's object set is `scene.objects` plus
every `addObject` minus every `removeObject` recorded so far" (see
`git show 797c037:src/core/resolve.ts`, which folded presence out of
reserved `obj+:`/`obj-:` flags). VOCAB.md v2 kept the two words and
dropped both mechanisms.

## 3. `removeObject: {scene, object, cause}` — implementable, but only by choosing

This one has exactly one possible home: rewrite `state.bundle.scenes[…]
.objects`, since `bundle` rides on GameState and `getView` derives the
object set from it. That works and is observable. But it is a one-way
delete — it destroys the definition, so the pair is asymmetric — and
`cause` still has no runtime home. The loader already requires `cause`
on the delta (`src/core/bundle.ts`), so "record their cause" is
satisfied at load; if the acceptance means observable at play, no field
and no `Effect` kind can carry it.

The v1 trick does not rescue this either: folding presence into
`state.flags` puts run-scoped, scene-scoped facts in the **campaign
ledger**, which death may not touch (RULES.md 9, GAME.md #ledgers) — a
drained pool would survive death — and it makes a reserved flag prefix
gate-visible to content, which is a new word in a closed language.

## which do you want?

1. **Amend the contract (H001 amendment card, before H006 re-runs).**
   GameState grows what the words need: a stat/tithe ledger with bounds
   on the run branch, and a world overlay recording per-scene object
   presence and the cause that changed it. Presence becomes
   catalogue-minus-absent, both affordance deltas become total and
   symmetric, `cause` gets a home. Cost: `src/core/types.ts` plus the
   shape walk in `src/core/save.ts` — neither in H006's scope. If
   `addObject` is to add anything not authored in place, H003's
   `CardObject` also needs its absent marker back.
2. **Surface instead of storing.** Add `adjust`, `addObject`,
   `removeObject` to the `Effect` union and let the shell own stats and
   object presence. Same file, same amendment card — and it puts game
   rules in the shell, which RULES.md 5 forbids.
3. **Narrow H006.** Cut the three words from this card's acceptance. The
   engine routes them through the exhaustive switch and applies nothing;
   they land with the systems that need them (the economy, the draw
   engine). Nothing is blocked by this: `content/shore.yaml` authors
   none of the three, and H111 authors none.

## what is not blocked

Everything else in the card is built-ready against the contract as
frozen, and I stopped rather than ship it half-guessed: first-passing-
gate selection and the mandatory gateless fallback; `say`, `set`,
`unset`, `give`, `take`, `journal`, `refuse`, `goto`, `prompt`, `fight`,
`end`; deltas in VOCAB.md order; the refused ledger and its purity rule
(one prose effect — the `say` **is** the refusal line); the free-tap law
against a structural deep clone; and the determinism replay.

Answer any of the three and this card finishes in one pass.

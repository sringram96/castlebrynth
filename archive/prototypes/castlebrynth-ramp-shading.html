the levers · v2

The dot pattern is fixed the way pixel art has always fixed it: one ramp per school, and dither only ever between two adjacent steps on it. Material, grime, light and fog all move a pixel along the same ramp instead of mixing arbitrary colour pairs.

Grammar rows now show a close crop of the wall, because those knobs are invisible at thumbnail size.

Still first-hit casting, still ordered dither, still deterministic, still no alpha and no gradients. One dither per pixel instead of four.

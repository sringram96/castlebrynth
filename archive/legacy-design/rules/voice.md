# The voice
Binding for every player-facing string. Content review is voice review.
Controls are exempt from this file's register and are governed by art. 66
in the-thumb.md.

**Amended by the mind wave (2026-08-06), which repeals the narrator.** The
old register described Castlebrynth with care — *the dark does the arguing* —
and the playtest of the reason wave found the flourish wrong. What replaces
it is below. The facts the reason wave delivered all stand; only the mouth
they come out of has changed.

## Whose voice this is

**The voice is his mind.** Every player-facing line is the protagonist
thinking or the protagonist writing — never a narrator. He is one plain,
frightened, determined man who came down here for his brother. Nothing on
screen is composed. It is had.

## The two voices, and both are his

**The thought** — live play. First person, present tense, plain words, short
lines. Fragments and questions are legal. Ellipses and dashes are
thought-speed, not decoration.

> Coins. Somebody's coins.
> That sound again. Closer this time.

**The scrawl** — anything written down: the waking line, the Book of Ends.
Second person imperative, because a note to yourself is an order. Lowercase
is legal in a scrawl; it was written badly, and fast.

> you must find him.
> don't sleep in the wet rooms.

A scrawl is short. He is out of time when he writes one.

## The rules

- **Plain man's words.** If a line sounds written, cut it. No lyricism, no
  metaphor he would not reach for. The old register's *"the dark does the
  arguing"* is exactly what this repeals.
- **Not-knowing is the device, not a style.** He names only what he knows.
  *What is that? …closer… oh no.* Recognition is knowledge, and knowledge is
  the thing that survives (arts 10, 34, 84 — now literal): an unmet horror is
  *"What— what IS that."* and a met one is its name: *"The Gnawing. I know
  this one."* The met-flags on the permanent ledger are what he still knows
  when everything else is gone.
- **Fear is had, never narrated.** *"oh no"* and *"no no no"* are legal.
  *"You feel dread"* stays banned, now for the true reason: he does not
  describe his feelings, he has them. *"I feel"* is banned for the same
  reason. The old ban on exclamation marks dies with the flourish — he is
  allowed one when it is earned, and never two.
- **The amnesia is canon.** Death is forgetting. The reseed is a way he
  cannot hold in his head. The permanent ledger is what is written down and
  what he still knows, so the fiction now *explains* the two ledgers
  (art. 11) instead of decorating them.
- **Prose never instructs the player.** He may urge himself — *"look
  closer"* is him — but no line tells the player what to press. Controls
  stay art. 66's: plain verbs, untouched by this file.

## Kept from the old law, because these were never the flourish

- **One candle at a time.** A beat is ~45 words or fewer.
- **The answer names the thing first** (art. 111, the-room.md) — *"A basin.
  Dry."* Naming is legibility, not style, so it survives the amendment
  whole. This file governs what comes after the noun.
- **Tappable nouns are concrete and singular.**
- **Nothing explains itself twice.**
- **Spoiler discipline is absolute.** Nothing states, hints, or rhymes with
  the depth-3 truth.

## The screens, and who speaks when the game is off
**Ruled 2026-08-07 by the mend's fourth wave.**

The settings screen and the vault had no voice because nobody had asked who
was speaking on them. They were declared placeholders and shipped in the
repealed register for three waves, which is what an unasked question looks
like in a content file.

**The answer is that on those two screens there is nobody in the room.** The
player is holding a phone. He has no thought about reduced motion, and a man
who narrated one would be the flourish this file repeals — so the settings
screen and the vault answer to the universals (no narrator, no feelings, no
shouting, one candle) and to **neither mouth**. That is the **plain**
category, and it is a decision rather than a debt. `placeholder` stays what
it is: prose that has not been rewritten yet, counted where it can be seen.

**Everything else on a screen is his**, and the wave found that most of it
already wanted to be. A fight he ran out of, a pouch he is carrying, a card
he is spending and a lock that will not give are as much his as a corridor
is; they were second person only because nobody had looked at them since the
narrator was repealed. **What a readout says is a label** — *sealed this
turn*, *already spent*, *on*, *off* — because it names a state and a name is
not a sentence (art. 90's reasoning, one level down).

## The lint

`src/content/voice.ts` is this file, executable, and
`test/content.voice.test.ts` is the review. Four categories:

- **thought** — prose in live play. Second person is a defect: it means a
  narrator is addressing the player.
- **scrawl** — anything written down. First person is a defect: a note to
  yourself is an order, not a diary.
- **label** — a name. Exempt from both registers, because a name is not a
  sentence.
- **plain** — the settings screen and the vault, where there is nobody in
  the room. The universals, and neither mouth. A decision, not a debt.
- **placeholder** — prose written under the repealed register and not yet
  rewritten. It answers only to the rules above that survive the
  amendment — the feeling ban, one candle, no narrator, no shouting — and
  it is a **debt with a name**, not a category anything new may be written
  in. Cards 27–29 empty it.

# The Lots — the dice
Articles keep their ledger numbers; cite as "art. N". Statuses as in
the-world.md. Reference encounter:
`reference/the-crawling-one-encounter.md` — the engine must reproduce it
turn for turn as a test.

## The turn
41. SETTLED — Yahtzee turns. A first casting, freeze any dice, one second
    casting of the rest, then the decision: THROW, BRACE, or FLEE. Two
    castings, every turn, free. Vocabulary is law: *freeze* is mid-turn
    keeping; *brace* is the defensive commit. Never the same word.
42. SETTLED — Intent first. The horror's intent is visible from the top of
    the turn, before the first casting; freezing is planning.
43. DEFAULT — The third casting is merchandise. Extra castings beyond the
    second exist only as scarce goods — spells, items, merchant wares.
44. DEFAULT — The hand returns whole each turn: throwing spends nothing;
    only effects may take a die.

## The duel
45. SETTLED — The poker duel. Dice are numbered; combos are sets and runs;
    harm = sum × tier. The Crawling One walkthrough is the reference
    encounter for turn feel and math.
46. SETTLED (amended) — Combos only: no set, no run — no THROW this turn.
    At six dice or more, a hand cannot whiff (values repeat, or six
    distinct is the great straight); below six, whiffing is possible.
    Losing dice below six is the true wound.
47. SETTLED — BRACE is valued: committed dice absorb their summed value.
    (Amends GAME.md's flat "eats one blow.")
48. DEFAULT — The ladder: sets ×count; runs ×(length−1); the great
    straight 1-2-3-4-5-6 is its own named tier. All numbers are tuning.

## The collection
49. SETTLED — Dice are collectible objects on four independent axes:
    shape, riders, bonds, talismans.
50. SETTLED — Shapes are free, values are law: any body (d4, d8, d20...),
    but every face shows a value 1–6. All value theorems survive any die
    ever forged; exotic distributions are declared on inspect.
51. SETTLED — The face schema: value + optional throw-rider + optional
    brace-rider. Riders fire only when the face is used; a frozen face
    fires nothing; passive faces are banned.
52. SETTLED (amended) — Bonds trigger when both dice are used in the same
    action at equal value. Carrying both halves in a finite hand is the
    bond's cost — structural, not bookkept.
53. SETTLED (amended) — Talismans (never "jokers" — renames GAME.md's
    term) are trinkets outside the hand that upgrade the scoring ladder
    in fun ways. They live as keepsakes on the permanent ledger.
54. SETTLED (amended) — Every power pays: everything declared on inspect;
    every die audited against the plain bone's power budget; every rare
    carries a cost face, a carry tax, or a price.
55. SETTLED — v1 ships six plain bones with all four sockets empty and
    their laws already in force.
56. DEFAULT — The signature is simply the first die you collect; a class
    is a curated starting hand. Classes stay parked; the socket is named.

## Display & intents
57. DEFAULT — Combat display: everything visible. The horror's health and
    intent sit at the top of the frame beside the word; your health, hand,
    and castings live in the tray.
58. DEFAULT — An intent is a declared verb + number + optional rider; the
    taxonomy of intents is content, authored per horror, not law.
60. SETTLED — The pouch and the hand. Your collection is the pouch, on
    the permanent ledger. The hand is assembled from it for the descent,
    and its size is not fixed: hand size is a body stat — grown by
    mercies, shrunk by wounds and curses. Composition is the build:
    throw-riders lean attack, brace-riders lean defense. v1 ships pouch
    of six plain bones, hand size six.

# The voice
Binding for every player-facing string. Content review is voice review.
Controls are exempt from this file's register and are governed by art. 66
in the-thumb.md.

- Second person, present tense.
- No "you feel." Name what is there; let the player do the feeling.
- No mercy, no jokes, no exclamation marks.
- One candle of text at a time: a beat is ~45 words or fewer.
- The labyrinth never explains itself twice.
- Tappable nouns are concrete and singular. A door's sense is one line —
  true, and incomplete.
- An answer names the thing first — *"A stone basin, dry."* — and only
  then says anything atmospheric (art. 111, the-room.md). Naming is
  legibility, not style: this file governs the sentence after the noun.

# Choosing screen — canonical visual contract

This screen is deliberately **not the reliquary**.

It exists only when the permanent pouch contains more dice than the hand can
carry. With the starting six bones and a hand size of six, it does not appear.

When it does appear:

- the background is black;
- every owned die is visible at once, arranged around an empty centre;
- each die is represented by its body/telling face, never by a six-face text row;
- tapping a die toggles that identity into or out of the hand;
- a selected die is physically raised upward and lit; no checkbox, border-box,
  count row, or menu cell is added;
- selection is capped at the hand size;
- **Confirm is absent until exactly the hand size is selected**;
- Confirm stands in the bottom-right safe area and begins the run;
- the die's tap still answers through the word band, as every free look does.

For larger collections, use a second concentric ring rather than scrolling or
turning the screen into a list. All choices remain on the table.

The old tap-tap-Swap / hand-above-rule / spare-list design is superseded. Do
not restore it from old comments, tests, or screenshots.
